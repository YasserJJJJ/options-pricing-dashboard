Model assumptions

European exercise, no dividends, constant volatility and risk-free interest rate, lognormal stock prices, and no transaction costs. Time to expiry uses a 365-day year. Payoff charts show a long option’s profit or loss at expiration, after subtracting the theoretical premium.

Monte Carlo intervals describe simulation uncertainty under these assumptions. They do not predict market prices.
